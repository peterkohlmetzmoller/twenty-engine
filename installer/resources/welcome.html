Twenty Engine

An 8-voice polysynth engine for the Korg MS-20 iC.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit for Logic, MainStage and GarageBand
  • VST3 for Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and others

Both run natively on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 11 or newer). Restart your music software after installing.
