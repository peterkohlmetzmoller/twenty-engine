Twenty Engine

An 8-voice polysynth engine for the Korg MS-20ic. This installs Twenty Engine Instrument: it plays like the hardware and keeps every recorded performance exactly as you played it.

It comes as an Audio Unit (Logic, MainStage, GarageBand) and a VST3 (Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and others), native on Intel and Apple Silicon, macOS 10.13 or newer. Restart your music software after installing.
