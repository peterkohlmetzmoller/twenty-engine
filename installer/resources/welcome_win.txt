Twenty Engine - an 8-voice polysynth engine for the Korg MS-20ic.

This installs the 64-bit VST3 plugin Twenty Engine Instrument into the standard VST3 folder (C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3). It plays like the hardware and keeps every recorded performance exactly as you played it.

It works in Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and other VST3 hosts. Your 36 memory sounds are kept in %APPDATA%\Twenty Engine and are not removed when you uninstall.

Performance recall needs the plugin to open the MS-20ic next to your DAW: Windows 11 with Windows MIDI Services allows that. On older Windows the plugin plays from the track as a normal instrument. For the panel LEDs, don't enable the MS-20ic as a MIDI output in your DAW.
