Twenty Engine - an 8-voice polysynth engine for the Korg MS-20 iC.

This installs the 64-bit VST3 plugin into the standard VST3 folder:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

It works in Ableton Live, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and other VST3 hosts. Your 36 memory sounds are kept in %APPDATA%\Twenty Engine and are not removed when you uninstall.
