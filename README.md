# Twenty Engine: free web edition

Twenty Engine in your browser: an 8-voice polysynth for the Korg MS-20ic, with the whole MS-20 panel,
patch cables and the iC's LEDs.

**Play it:** https://peterkohlmetzmoller.github.io/twenty-engine/ (Chrome or Edge on a computer)

- Plug in the MS-20ic (USB) or any MIDI keyboard, click START and allow MIDI (and SysEx: it lights the iC's LEDs).
- No keyboard? The computer's keys play it: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, with Z and X for the octave.
- Sounds you save on the keys and learned MIDI controllers stay in your browser.
